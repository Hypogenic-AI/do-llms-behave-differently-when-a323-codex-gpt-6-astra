## Search strategy and decisions

The paper-finder diligent search returned 39 candidates; 12 met the task-specific relevance screen and were downloaded. Explicit resources were prioritized, followed by primary arXiv/publisher pages and citation chasing. [Screening log](notes/search_screening.md) records all candidates and exclusions; raw service results are under `paper_search_results/`. Semantic Scholar metadata calls returned HTTP 429, so primary sources supplied metadata. An MDPI PDF initially returned 403; its publisher CDN succeeded. All specified resources are represented; large datasets were deliberately limited to relevant subsets.

Exactly three research directions were retained after scoring evidence, relevance, information gain and feasibility: **paired style effects; crossed source labels; controlled representation interventions**. The ranking and five rejected alternatives are documented in [planning.md](planning.md). No later expansion occurred. Code selection supports those directions; observational origin comparisons and detector leaderboards are not separate research arms.

## Experiment handoff

1. Build proposition-audited style pairs from JBB and a grouped, stratified verified Anthropic subset. WildChat supplies wording examples, not provenance truth. Freeze inclusion, semantic-equivalence and deduplication rules before outcome generation.
2. Cross identical bodies with human/AI/no source labels. Keep certainty, grammatical person, politeness, task details and harm level fixed or explicitly ablate them. Use multiple writers and independent responder sessions.
3. Measure harmful compliance separately from benign over-refusal; report opinion agreement, invalid responses, paired percentage-point changes and clustered confidence intervals. Blind judges and validate a human-audited subset. Do not infer factual accuracy from opinion labels.
4. If the behavioral design is sound, adapt MGT probes and format controls. Hold out request/generator families, fit preprocessing on training only, and compare random/nuisance directions, ablation and rescue. Separability or steering alone is insufficient evidence of mediation.

The fresh project `.venv`, [pyproject.toml](pyproject.toml) and `uv.lock` contain resource-gathering dependencies only. [Environment evidence](notes/environment_validation.json) records one RTX A6000 with 49,140 MiB; a 7–8B pilot is plausible but untested. Model weights, activation datasets, generator access, judges, runtime dependencies and actual cost estimates remain experiment-phase work. Use the 1,200-output JBB style pilot and 3,600-output crossed design as planning counts, not a completed or statistically powered study.


## Research execution appendix (2026-10-04)

Implemented the same three ranked directions using the gathered JBB, verified Anthropic and WildChat files. No observational corpus-vs-corpus comparison or detector-only research direction was added. Exact-content format control and a stricter audit were added after rewrite-control failures; all amendments are preserved in notes/planning_full_execution.md.

Primary API models were verified against the live OpenRouter catalog: google/gemini-3.1-flash-lite and qwen/qwen3.5-9b. qwen/qwen3.5-397b-a17b served writing/judging roles. Local Qwen/Qwen3-8B revision b968826d9c46dd6066d109eabc6255188de91218 was used only as an older mechanistic baseline. The isolated environment, exact dependencies and GPU runtime are in results/environment.json, uv.lock and results/local_runtime.json.

Experiments used 180 benchmark requests and 100 independent calibration requests. Rewrites succeeded on 124 benchmark requests, but only seven passed strict full-detail controls. The narrow exact-content formatting manipulation changed Gemini source judgments strongly; primary behavioral comparisons were not significant after correction. Local representation separability was largely predicted by measured surface features, and steering did not establish a specific internal-authorship mechanism. These are qualified findings, not a universal null.
